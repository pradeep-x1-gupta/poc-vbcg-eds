About Value-based Care Guides

Value-based care guides (VBCGs) are tools developed to assist clinicians in delivering care that is both evidence-based and aligned with value-based care principles. Each guide is crafted with input from leading clinicians and incorporates the latest research, clinical guidelines, and best practices.

VBCGs are not a substitute for the independent clinical judgment of healthcare practitioners. Instead, they serve as valuable tools that complement clinical expertise, enabling providers to make informed decisions tailored to each patient's specific circumstances.
